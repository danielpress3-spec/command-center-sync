Privacy Policy

Command Center Sync is a personal, single-user tool operated by Daniel Press.

What data is accessed

The app accesses only Daniel Press's own Google Tasks, using his own Google account. It does not access any other user's data.

How the data is used

Google Tasks data is used only to sync tasks with Daniel Press's own Obsidian vault: copying tasks that have due dates into a Google Tasks list and keeping their completion status in sync.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with, or disclosed to anyone.
